## Section A: Store Purchase Rules

### 1. Our department needs to buy 50 laptops. What should we do first?

First consolidate the requirement: number of laptops, users, purpose, delivery timeline, budget head, and estimated value. Then prepare generic technical specifications, confirm budget availability, get competent administrative/financial approval, and check whether suitable laptops are available on GeM or another approved channel. Only after this should the department choose the permitted procurement method and create the tender or GeM bid.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 2. Can our department purchase laptops directly from GeM?

Yes, if laptops with the required specifications are available on GeM and the current GeM/GFR and Chhattisgarh rules permit that mode for the value and circumstances. Direct purchase is not automatic for every case; the department must check the applicable monetary threshold, compare price/reasonableness, verify specifications, and record the approval and justification.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rule 3; GFR/GeM provisions.

### 3. We need to buy office furniture. Which procurement method should we use?

Use the method permitted after checking the estimated value, availability on GeM/approved state channels, and delegated powers. If the item is available on GeM and conditions permit, use GeM. If not, use tendering under the Store Purchase Rules. Do not choose limited tender, single tender, or direct purchase only for convenience.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rules 3 and 4.

### 4. What is the difference between Open Tender and Limited Tender?

Open Tender is publicly advertised and allows all eligible bidders to participate. Limited Tender is sent only to a restricted list of known/registered/capable suppliers and can be used only when the rules permit it. Open Tender gives wider competition; Limited Tender is a restricted method and needs proper justification and adequate competition among invited firms.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; CHiPS Bid Submission Manual tender type definitions.

### 5. When is Single Tender allowed?

Single Tender is an exceptional method where an offer is obtained from one identified source. It is generally allowed only for rule-permitted situations such as proprietary/single-source items, standardization or compatibility requirements, or justified urgency/emergency, with written reasons and competent approval. It should not be used merely because it is faster.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 6. Department ko 100 chairs kharidni hain. Tender karna padega ya direct purchase ho sakti hai?

Pehle total requirement aur estimated value calculate karein. Agar chairs GeM ya approved channel par available hain aur value ke hisab se direct purchase allowed hai, to direct purchase ho sakti hai. Agar conditions direct purchase allow nahi karti, to bidding/tender process use karein. Requirement ko chhote orders mein split karke direct purchase nahi karna chahiye.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; GFR/GeM provisions.

### 7. Can one government department purchase goods from another government department?

Yes, the Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules include provisions for purchase from other departments and their undertakings, and for one department/undertaking purchasing from another. The department should still record the authority, rates/terms, requirement, approval, and delivery/acceptance process.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rules 8 and 9.

### 8. Emergency situation mein department purchase kaise kare?

Emergency mein bhi file record banana zaroori hai: emergency ka nature, delay se hone wala risk, required quantity, estimate, and approval. Natural disaster ya law-and-order type urgent situations ke liye rules special procurement route allow karte hain, but competent authority approval and reasoned justification should be recorded. Emergency ko routine bypass nahi banana chahiye.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rule 10; procurement vigilance guidance.

### 9. Before purchasing computers, what approvals are required?

The department should obtain need approval/indent approval, budget availability confirmation, administrative approval, financial sanction as per delegated powers, and approval of the procurement method. Technical specifications should also be approved or vetted by competent technical personnel before tender/GeM action.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 10. Purchase order issue hone ke baad next process kya hota hai?

Vendor ko PO/contract ke terms ke according supply karni hoti hai. Department delivery receive karega, quantity and specifications verify karega, inspection/testing karega, defects ho to record karega, and formal acceptance/Goods Receipt and Inspection Report banayega. Acceptance ke baad invoice processing, payment, and stock/asset register update hota hai.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rule 11.

## Section B: Department Buyer Workflow

### 11. Mujhe department ke liye printer kharidna hai. Process batao.

Requirement define karein, quantity and purpose record karein, generic specifications banayein, estimated cost and budget confirm karein, approval lein, GeM/approved channel availability check karein, permitted method select karein, then GeM bid/direct purchase or tender create karein. Bids/offers evaluate karke competent approval ke baad PO issue karein, delivery inspect karein, then payment process karein.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 12. Department ko software license purchase karna hai. Kya process rahega?

Need and scope define karein: license type, number of users, subscription period, support, data/security requirements, and renewal terms. Avoid brand/vendor lock-in unless justified. Check GeM/approved sources, estimate cost, obtain approvals, use the permitted tender/GeM method, evaluate technical compliance and price, issue PO/contract, and keep license/asset records.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules.

### 13. How should technical specifications for laptops be prepared?

Specifications should be functional, generic, measurable, and competition-friendly. Mention required processor class/performance, RAM, storage, display size/resolution, operating system requirement if justified, warranty, service support, and delivery terms. Avoid unnecessary restrictive conditions and avoid copying one brand's catalogue.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; CVC guidance on fair specifications.

### 14. Can we specify Dell laptops only?

Normally, no. A department should not specify only Dell or any single brand unless there is a documented, rule-permitted technical/proprietary/compatibility reason and competent approval. The safer approach is to write generic specifications and allow equivalent compliant products.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; CVC guidance.

### 15. Department ko AC units ka AMC karana hai. Procedure kya hai?

AMC requirement define karein: number/type of ACs, sites, service frequency, response time, spare parts scope, contract period, penalty/SLA terms, and payment terms. Estimate value, confirm budget, check GeM/service availability, select the permitted method, invite bids/GeM bid if required, evaluate technical and financial offers, issue contract, and monitor service performance.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 16. Can we split a purchase into smaller orders?

No, not to avoid approval limits, tendering requirements, GeM thresholds, or higher authority sanction. The requirement should be consolidated for the same/similar items over the relevant period. Quantity splitting is acceptable only when disclosed and justified for genuine supply/security reasons, not to bypass procurement rules.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; CVC guidance.

### 17. Department ko 20 desktop computers urgently chahiye. Kya options hain?

Urgency ko document karein. First check GeM/direct purchase/L1/bidding options under applicable thresholds. If direct purchase is not permitted, use faster permissible bidding/tender route or emergency/special route only where the rules and competent approval allow it. Do not use single tender merely because the department planned late.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules; GFR/GeM provisions.

### 18. Bid evaluation kaise hoti hai?

Evaluation published tender criteria ke against hoti hai. First eligibility and document compliance check hota hai, then technical responsiveness/specification compliance. Sirf technically responsive bids ki financial evaluation honi chahiye. Price reasonableness, preferences/exemptions if applicable, and committee recommendations should be recorded before award.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules.

### 19. Lowest bidder select karna compulsory hai kya?

Lowest price alone enough nahi hai. L1 normally wahi bidder hota hai jo technically and commercially responsive bhi ho. Agar lowest bidder non-responsive, ineligible, or non-compliant hai, to usko select karna compulsory nahi hai. Rejection reasons and evaluation record properly document hone chahiye.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 20. Inspection aur acceptance process kya hota hai?

Delivery ke baad department quantity, model/specification, warranty, documents, and quality ko PO/contract ke against verify karta hai. Testing/inspection required ho to conduct hota hai. Conforming goods ko formal Goods Receipt and Inspection Report/acceptance ke through accept kiya jata hai; defects/short supply ko record karke rectification/replacement action liya jata hai.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rule 11.

## Section C: Vendor Registration and Participation

### 21. How do I register as a vendor?

Go to the CHiPS e-Procurement portal, use New Supplier Registration/New User, enter PAN and business details, choose preferred login code, fill authorized signatory/contact/bank/vendor details, upload required documents such as CRN where applicable, select the DSC, accept terms, and generate the vendor registration/application number. After approval and payment, reset password and start bidding.

Source basis: CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual.

### 22. New supplier registration ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?

New supplier ke liye PAN card information mandatory hai. Vendor Class A/B/C/D ke liye CRN certificate scanned copy required hai. Preferred login code, authorized signatory/contact details, bank details, business coordinates, and valid DSC are also required. Existing/PWD registered suppliers may need enrollment/PWD details.

Source basis: CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual.

### 23. Vendor password bhool gaya hoon. Kya karun?

Portal ke login page par Password Recovery option use karein. Recovery email/DSC/OTP route available hai. Required user details enter karein, DSC ya OTP se validation karein, then new password create karein. If the account is blocked or details do not match, contact CHiPS/helpdesk.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual; CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual.

### 24. DSC kaise obtain karun?

Class II or Class III signing/encryption DSC kisi licensed Certifying Authority se obtain karein. Manual me e-Mudhra, GNFC/nCode, IDRBT, MTNL Trustline, NIC, and Safescrypt jaise CAs listed hain. DSC ke bina secure e-Procurement login and tender participation possible nahi hota.

Source basis: CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual; CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 25. Renewed DSC ko portal par map kaise karun?

Renewed DSC token drivers and required setup install karein, portal par login/DSC registration or digital certificate option open karein, appropriate certificate select karein, and confirm/map it with the registered user account. Bid submit karne se pehle ensure karein ki valid DSC correctly selected and accepted hai.

Source basis: CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual; CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 26. Can a startup participate in tenders?

Yes. A startup can participate if it meets the tender conditions. Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules mention benefits for valid Government-recognized startups in specified cases, such as relaxation from prior experience and turnover requirements, subject to the conditions in the tender/rules. Startup status does not waive all eligibility, EMD, technical, or submission requirements automatically.

Source basis: Chhattisgarh Store Purchase Rules rule 4.2; CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 27. Foreign company tender mein participate kaise kare?

Foreign company ko tender eligibility first check karni chahiye. Portal participation ke liye valid DSC required hai. Foreign vendors can obtain Class II/III DSC through licensed CA process involving application, documents certified by Indian Embassy, payment to CA, and issuance of DSC/e-token. Tender-specific foreign bidder conditions must also be followed.

Source basis: CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual; CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 28. Technical bid aur financial bid mein kya difference hai?

Technical bid me eligibility documents, credentials, technical compliance, specifications, experience, certificates, and required attachments submit hote hain. Financial bid/BOQ me quoted prices/rates submit hote hain. Normally technical bid pehle evaluate hoti hai; financial bid only technically qualified bidders ke liye open/evaluate hoti hai.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 29. Tender eligibility criteria kaise check karun?

Portal par tender/NIT details open karein and eligibility, vendor category, required documents, technical experience, financial turnover, bid capacity, EMD, dates, attachments, and corrigenda check karein. Restricted/limited tenders me only eligible/specified categories or invited vendors participate kar sakte hain.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 30. Vendor registration approve hone mein kitna time lagta hai?

The manual does not give a fixed approval time. Registration is completed after application submission, scrutiny/approval, intimation for registration payment, payment, and first-login password reset. Vendor should monitor portal/email and contact CHiPS/helpdesk if approval/payment intimation is delayed.

Source basis: CHiPS Vendor Registration Manual.

## Section D: EMD and Bid Submission

### 31. I need to pay Rs 50,000 EMD by 15 June 2026. How do I do it?

Login to the CHiPS portal, open the relevant tender, go to payment/EMD section, confirm that the tender shows Rs 50,000 EMD and the 15 June 2026 deadline, choose the allowed payment mode such as eChallan/RTGS/NEFT or internet banking/card if enabled, generate/upload challan where required, sign/submit payment details, verify the status, and download/keep the receipt. Bid submission is allowed only after successful payment status in the system.

Source basis: EMD Challan Payment Manual.

### 32. EMD payment fail ho gaya but paisa debit ho gaya. Kya karun?

Portal me Unsuccessful Transaction/Refresh or Verify option se status check karein. Agar eChallan payment bid due date tak beneficiary account me credit nahi hota, system may treat EMD as not paid and bid can be rejected; amount is refunded back to source account. Helpdesk/bank/department ko payment reference, tender number, challan/transaction number, and debit proof ke saath immediately contact karein before bid deadline.

Source basis: EMD Challan Payment Manual.

### 33. EMD refund kab milega?

Unsuccessful bidders ke liye tender owner/department admin bid opening/evaluation stage ke baad EMD refund/remittance process initiate karta hai. Department approver verification/approval ke baad amount registered bank account me credit hota hai. Notice ke mutabik department approval ke 1 or 2 days ke andar credit expected hai, and refund MIS portal par available hota hai.

Source basis: Online EMD Refund Notice.

### 34. L1 bidder ki EMD ka kya hota hai?

L1/successful bidder ki EMD normal unsuccessful bidder refund flow me immediately release nahi hoti. Department has options: remit L1 EMD to department or refund it to L1 bidder after conditions such as receipt of Performance Bank Guarantee are fulfilled. Tender owner/department admin initiates the applicable process.

Source basis: Online EMD Refund Notice; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 35. Kya MSME ko EMD exemption mil sakta hai?

Yes, MSE/MSME exemption may be available where the applicable tender/GFR/MSE procurement policy provides it and the bidder submits valid registration/certificates. Exemption is not automatic for every tender or every MSME; bidder must check the tender clause and upload required exemption proof.

Source basis: GFR updated document; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 36. Bid submit karne ka complete process batao.

Register/login as vendor with valid DSC, search/select tender, read NIT and corrigenda, check eligibility and dates, pay tender fee/EMD if applicable, fill techno-commercial bid, upload required attachments, sign with DSC, fill financial/BOQ/price bid, sign and encrypt/submit as required, verify successful submission, and save acknowledgement/receipt. Submit well before deadline.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual; EMD Challan Payment Manual.

### 37. Can I modify my bid after submission?

Before the bid submission deadline, modification/substitution/withdrawal may be possible as allowed by the portal and tender conditions; the latest valid submitted bid is normally considered. After any modification, the bidder must ensure final submission status is successful before the deadline.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 38. Bid deadline ke baad bid edit kar sakte hain kya?

No. Bid due date/time ke baad bid edit ya submit nahi karna chahiye and portal usually does not allow it. Local internet, browser, DSC, or system problems at bidder side are not treated as portal malfunction, so bid deadline se pehle submit karna bidder ki responsibility hai.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 39. Technical bid submit ho gayi hai. Financial bid kaise submit karun?

Same tender workflow me price bid/financial bid/BOQ section open karein, quoted rates or BOQ values fill/upload karein as required, validate totals, sign/encrypt with DSC, save/submit, and verify final bid submission status. Technical and financial parts must both be submitted before deadline unless the tender specifically uses a different process.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.

### 40. How do I participate in an e-auction?

Login to the Chhattisgarh e-Procurement portal with user ID, password, and DSC. Open the Auction tab in a new tab/window, change auction password if prompted, select the relevant auction, click View/Respond to RFx, place bids above opening price and in multiples of the minimum bid-change value, submit bid, monitor H1/ranking during the auction, and view result after close.

Source basis: Auction Manual.

## Section E: Department Operator / Portal Operations

### 41. How do I create a tender on the portal?

Login as authorized department/tender creator, open Tender module, choose Add/Create Tender or Offline Tender where applicable, enter tender header details such as tender number, NIT reference, description, PAC, bid parts, tender category, department/division, bid start/due/open dates, fee/EMD and attachment requirements, save, route for approval where required, and publish only after verification.

Source basis: Manual Offline Tenders; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 42. Department operator tender publish kaise kare?

Tender draft complete hone ke baad NIT, dates, bid parts, documents, EMD/fees, and approvals verify karein. Workflow/approver clearance ke baad publish action use karein, DSC/authorization complete karein, and check that tender is visible on portal with correct dates and documents. Incorrect dates/documents should be corrected before publishing.

Source basis: Manual Offline Tenders; CHiPS portal manuals.

### 43. Offline tender upload kaise karte hain?

Login as Tender Creator, go to Tender module and select Offline Tender. Manual/offline tender header details enter karein: organization, tender dealing officer, tender no., NIT reference, tender call no., description, PAC, division/circle/office, bid parts, tender category, bid submission start date, bid due date, bid open date, and required document details. Save/sign/submit as per workflow.

Source basis: Manual Offline Tenders.

### 44. Technical bids kaise open karte hain?

Authorized department user scheduled bid open date/time ke baad portal me login karta hai, relevant tender selects, Part-I/technical opening option use karta hai, required DSC/decryption steps complete karta hai, and submitted technical bids/documents ko open/download/scrutinize karta hai. Opening report/attendance/system record should be preserved.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 45. Price bid opening process kya hai?

Technical evaluation complete hone ke baad only technically responsive/qualified bidders ki price bid open honi chahiye. Authorized user scheduled Part-II opening process me login/DSC/decryption use karta hai, price bids/BOQ opens, comparative statement/system reports generate karta hai, and evaluation committee rates and L1 determination records karti hai.

Source basis: CHiPS Bid Submission Manual; Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024.

### 46. How does a department issue a corrigendum?

Department user dashboard se relevant tender search/open karta hai, required corrigendum type choose karta hai: header, date, EMD/bid security, tender term, attachment, required attachment, or item. Changes save karein, bid-deletion impact carefully review karein, Publish All Corrigendum/Addendum choose karein, and approver workflow/DSC approval complete hone ke baad corrigendum published hota hai.

Source basis: CHiPS Corrigendum Issuance Manual.

### 47. Tender ki last date extend karni hai. Kya process hai?

Date Corrigendum create karein. Relevant tender open karke Add Tender Date Corrigendum select karein, revised bid due date/opening dates enter karein, save karein, bid-deletion option/impact review karein, publish workflow route karein, and approver DSC approval ke baad corrigendum publish ho jayega. Bidders should then follow revised dates.

Source basis: CHiPS Corrigendum Issuance Manual.

### 48. EMD refund process department operator kaise initiate kare?

Tender owner/department admin applicable stage ke baad unsuccessful bidders ke liye EMD refund/remittance process initiate karta hai. Department approver verification/approval karta hai, then system registered bidder bank accounts me refund process karta hai. L1 bidder ke liye separate L1 EMD remittance/refund option use hota hai depending on contract/PBG status.

Source basis: Online EMD Refund Notice.

### 49. Corrigendum upload karne ke baad bidders ko kya karna hota hai?

Bidders ko View Corrigendum/Addendum se changes check karne chahiye, revised dates/terms/documents/EMD/items verify karne chahiye, and portal par dekhna chahiye ki earlier bid deleted hai ya valid. Agar bid deleted hai, fresh bid submit karein. Required Attachment Corrigendum me attachments delete ho sakti hain, so fresh attachments resubmit karna zaroori ho sakta hai.

Source basis: CHiPS Corrigendum Issuance Manual.

### 50. Department admin bid evaluation report kaise generate kare?

Technical opening and scrutiny ke baad portal/system reports, bid opening record, compliance sheets, technical evaluation, price comparative statement, and committee minutes generate/compile karein. Evaluation published criteria ke against honi chahiye. Report me responsive/non-responsive bidders, reasons for rejection, L1/rate reasonableness, preferences/exemptions, and award recommendation record honi chahiye.

Source basis: Manual for Procurement of Goods 2024; CHiPS Bid Submission Manual.
