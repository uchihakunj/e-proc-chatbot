# Sample Questions by Document

A trilingual test bank for the CHiPS e-Procurement chatbot — **5 questions per document**,
covering **English [EN]**, **Hindi [HI]**, and **Hinglish [HIN]** (romanized Hindi).
Distribution per document: 2 English, 2 Hindi, 1 Hinglish (so all three languages appear for every doc).

> Tip: these double as a regression set. The bot expands Hindi/Hinglish terms to their English
> document-terms at retrieval time, so equivalent questions across languages should reach the same source.

### Notes
- **PPM 00002**, **publicProManual**, **PEF**, and the **Précis** are process/portal documents whose exact
  titles are opaque; their questions target the procurement-process content they contain. If a question
  retrieves a different (but topically correct) manual, that is expected — the comprehensive manuals
  legitimately answer generic process questions.
- For a quick smoke test, try one question per language from documents 1–3 and 13–14 (EMD, goods,
  bid submission, vendor registration) — these have the cleanest, most direct source content.
